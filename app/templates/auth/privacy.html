Privacy Policy

Clarity Grader does not sell your information. It is not read, shared or changed except in the narrow cases described below. This policy covers claritygrader.net and is run by Brody Estes ("Operator"), who maintains the site in his own name.

It applies to instructors who use the site and to the student records those instructors enter. Students do not have accounts and never log in.

What information is collected

  • Instructor account: name, email address and login details.
  • Class records an instructor enters: class names, assignments and learning objectives, student names, grades and the history of grade changes, homework scores and homework pass usage, and the settings for each class.
  • Student email addresses: instructors enter an email address for each student on a class roster. It is used for the progress and revision emails the instructor sends to that student.
  • Technical data: a session cookie that keeps an instructor logged in, a few settings stored in the browser (the display theme and session-timeout timers), and standard server logs used to find and fix errors.
  • Payment details: payments are made to Operator through Venmo, outside this website. The site does not collect, see or store payment or card information.

How the information is used

Information is used only to run Clarity Grader for the instructor who entered it: to record and show grades, produce reports, exports and archives, send the progress and revision emails the instructor asks for, send account emails such as password resets, keep accounts secure and fix problems.

Never sold

Operator does not sell, rent or trade any information on this site. It is not used for advertising, marketing profiles or tracking, and it is not shared with anyone except the service providers listed below, who need it to run the site.

Who can see it

An instructor can see only the classes they own. Operator does not read class records as a matter of course. Operator looks at specific records only when needed to:

  • help with a problem the instructor reported,
  • find and repair a technical fault, or
  • meet a legal obligation.

When Operator looks at an instructor's records for one of these reasons, it is limited to what that reason needs.

Service providers

Operator uses outside providers that store or process data only to run the site:

  • Railway: hosts the website
  • Supabase: stores the database and handles login
  • Resend: sends email, including progress and revision emails to students at an instructor's request and account emails to instructors, such as password reset and password-changed notices

These providers hold data on Operator's behalf under their own security and privacy terms. They are not permitted by Operator to use it for their own purposes.

Integrity and security

Operator does not change grades or class records. The only exceptions are a change an instructor asks for, or repairing a technical fault, and in both cases Operator tells the instructor.

Safeguards in place:

  • The site is served over HTTPS.
  • Every page with class data requires login, and an instructor can open only their own classes.
  • Operator keeps the software and hosting up to date.

No website can promise perfect security. If Operator learns of a breach affecting an instructor's data, Operator tells that instructor promptly.

Keeping, exporting and deleting data

Data is kept while an instructor's account is active. Instructors can export their class data at any time as CSV files and class archives.

An instructor can ask Operator to delete their account and class data at brodyestes4@icloud.com. Operator deletes it from the live system within 30 days and from backups within 90 days after that.

Instructors and student records

Instructors decide what student information to enter and are responsible for having the right to do so under their institution's policies and applicable law, including FERPA where it applies. Because students have no accounts, a student who wants to see or correct a record should ask their instructor, who can change it directly.

Cookies

The site uses a session cookie to keep an instructor logged in and a security token to protect forms from forgery. It also keeps a few settings in the browser's local storage, such as the display theme and session-timeout timers. It does not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes in a way that matters, Operator emails instructors at least 30 days before the change takes effect. Last updated: November 14, 2026.

Questions, access requests and deletion requests: brodyestes4@icloud.com.
